## Inventory Management Requirements

### Physical Books
- [ ] Stock decreases on order CONFIRM (not on checkout)
- [ ] Stock restored on order CANCEL
- [ ] Stock cannot go negative
- [ ] Oversell prevention (10 users, 5 stock = 5 orders max)

### Digital Books
- [ ] No stock limit
- [ ] Unlimited copies can be sold
- [ ] Access granted on order COMPLETED

### Hybrid Books (BOTH)
- [ ] Physical portion has stock
- [ ] Digital portion unlimited
- [ ] Can order physical-only, digital-only, or both
